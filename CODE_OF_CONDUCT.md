# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes respectful, evidence-based collaboration about Nokia Beacon WebUI behavior and owner-authorized diagnostics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Describe observations, firmware context, and limitations clearly.
- Protect private network data and other people’s information.
- Keep testing scoped to devices you own or are explicitly authorized to administer.
- Prefer reproducible, read-only evidence before proposing a configuration change.
- Accept corrections when a result is incomplete or firmware-specific.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing passwords, tokens, PSKs, private keys, serial numbers, private MAC addresses, or customer identifiers.
- Instructions for credential theft, brute force, remote authentication bypass, or unauthorized access.
- Deliberately destructive tests against a device or network.
- Misrepresenting a shared Nokia frontend string as proof of support on every device.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect people, devices, or private data.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, do not publish secrets or a weaponized proof of concept in a public issue. Use GitHub’s private security reporting workflow when available.
